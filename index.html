Evolution & Rebirth
From Pro Musician in China to Spiritual Digital Monk
My Story

When I started playing guitar at age six, I never dreamed music would take me around the world ten times over—touring the USA, Europe, South America, and living a decade in Asia as founder of the China Blues band and Foreign Music Manager in Shenzhen.

Despite performing at six-star resorts overlooking Hong Kong Bay and private events for billionaires, I felt an empty void seeing extreme poverty right outside those exclusive venues. I walked away from the music business, renounced performing, and spent years studying in monasteries and temples across China and Southeast Asia before returning to Chapel Hill to care for my mother during her battle with cancer.

In February 2022, just before the Russia-Ukraine war began, I heard an interview with a Ukrainian jazz singer who said she coped with the coming war by losing herself in music—while an incredible trumpet played in the background. Having left all my guitars in China, I pulled out a trumpet gifted by a close friend in Shenzhen, taught myself on the steps of UNC's Kenan Music Building, and lowered my ego right out onto the street corner to serve others.

“I am not a musician, I am a monk that plays music. Some people bring food to help others and some people bring blankets. For me, the best gift I could give was music.” — Chris (Trumpet Monk)
